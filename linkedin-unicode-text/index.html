What Is Unicode Text?

Unicode is a character encoding standard that assigns a unique number to every character used in digital text. It includes not just regular letters A-Z, but also mathematical symbols that look like bold, italic, or script versions of those letters.

These mathematical alphanumeric symbols are part of the Unicode standard and render as styled text in any modern browser or app, including LinkedIn.

Example: The letter "A" has multiple Unicode representations:

Bold:𝐀
Italic:𝐴
Script:𝒜
Double:𝔸

Accessibility Note

Screen readers may read Unicode mathematical symbols letter-by-letter rather than as words. Use for short emphasis only. Keep formatting to 1-3 words for names, titles, and callouts. Avoid formatting full paragraphs.
