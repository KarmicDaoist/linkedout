How to Make Text Bold in LinkedIn Posts

LinkedIn does not have a rich-text editor. Here is how to add bold, italic, and styled text to your LinkedIn posts using Unicode characters.

The Problem With LinkedIn Formatting

LinkedIn posts only accept plain text. There is no way to select text and click "bold" or "italic" like you can in Word or Google Docs. This is a deliberate limitation of the platform.

The solution is Unicode bold characters. These are letters that look bold because of how they are encoded, not because of any font. When you paste them into LinkedIn, they display as bold text.

How Unicode Bold Works

Unicode includes a set of mathematical bold letters. Regular ASCII letters have bold counterparts in the Unicode character set:

Regular text:
Hiring for senior engineers
Unicode bold:

The bold version uses 𝐀 (U+1D400) instead of A. They look identical in a text editor but display as bold in browsers and social media.
