How Auto-Conversion Works

When you paste text from ChatGPT into LinkedOut, it automatically detects and converts these markdown patterns:

**bold** → 𝐛𝐨𝐥𝐝
__bold__ → 𝐛𝐨𝐥𝐝
_italic_ → 𝐢𝐭𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐜
*italic* → 𝐢𝐭𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐜
~~strike~~ → s̶t̶r̶i̶k̶e̶
`code` → 𝚌𝚘𝚍𝚎

Copied from ChatGPT:
We are **hiring** a Senior Software Engineer! Requirements: - 5+ years of _Python_ experience - Experience with **cloud platforms** (AWS or GCP) - Strong communication skills ~~This role is remote~~ Remote-first culture! Check out our tech stack: `React`, `Node.js`, `PostgreSQL`
↓
Pasted to LinkedIn:
We are 𝐡𝐢𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 a Senior Software Engineer! Requirements: - 5+ years of 𝐼𝑡𝑎𝑙𝑖𝑐 experience - Experience with **cloud platforms** (AWS or GCP) - Strong communication skills T̶h̶i̶s̶ ̶r̶o̶l̶e̶ ̶i̶s̶ ̶r̶e̶m̶o̶t̶e̶ Remote-first culture! Check out our tech stack: 𝚁𝚎𝚊𝚌𝚝, 𝙽𝚘𝚍𝚎.𝚓𝚜, 𝙿𝚘𝚜𝚝𝚐𝚛𝚎𝚂𝚀𝙻

What Gets Converted

  • ✓ Bold text: **text** or __text__
  • ✓ Italic text: *text* or _text_
  • ✓ Strikethrough: ~~text~~
  • ✓ Inline code: `code`
  • ✓ Code blocks: Triple-backtick blocks are converted to monospace

Screen Reader Note

Screen readers may read Unicode mathematical symbols letter-by-letter. Use bold and italic styles for short emphasis only — names, job titles, or callouts — and avoid formatting full paragraphs.
